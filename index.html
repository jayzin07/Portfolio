About

I'm a data analyst with hands-on experience driving marketing and business decisions for one of Brazil's largest fast-food chains. I use SQL Server, Python and Power BI to build automation pipelines, dashboards and machine-learning customer segmentation models (K-Means). I've delivered a CRM covering over 450K customers and a cashback dashboard tracking R$2.16M in accumulated balance.

My work has always been in data analytics — what changed was the business around it. I've applied data in civil aviation (ANAC), financial markets (VLGI Investimentos), online education (Inception), credit securitization (Ativos S.A.) and now food retail at Giraffas, each with its own questions, metrics and stakeholders. That range taught me to start from the business problem and turn it into analysis leadership can act on. I hold a Production Engineering degree from the University of Brasília and have a growing interest in applied AI, like the RAG-based assistant I'm prototyping in the franchisee portal.
