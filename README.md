## About

My work centers on automated pipelines, query optimization and dashboards that replace manual work and give leadership a clear basis for decisions. Production Engineering graduate from the University of Brasília, with exposure to machine learning (Random Forest, neural networks, k-means clustering).

- **Co-branding ROI Analysis** — comparison of dessert partnerships that informed a menu decision.
- **Cashback Program Dashboard** — the program's first Power BI dashboard, replacing an unused spreadsheet; filtered answers in under 10 minutes.
- **Customer CRM Dashboard** — customer profiling by gender, age range and region over a 450K-customer base.
- **Support Ticket Triage Dashboard** — filters by date, time to due date, subject and urgency for ~45 tickets/month.
